Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Stash helps you save links to posts and web pages and keep them organized. This policy explains what information Stash handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: Stash doesn’t sell your data, doesn’t show ads, and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites.

01 Information Stored on Your Device

When you share a post or link to Stash, the app stores the following on your iPhone:

  • The link (URL).
  • Any text shared along with it, such as a caption.
  • A title and preview image for the link.
  • The categories you create.

Stash fetches the title and preview image directly from the linked website. This data stays on your device unless you turn on auto-sorting (see Section 02). Deleting a save, or deleting the app, removes this data from your device.

02 Auto-Sorting (Optional, Requires Sign-In)

If you choose to sign in, Stash can sort new saves into your categories automatically and write a short description of each one. To do this, Stash sends the following to our server:

  • The saved link and any shared caption.
  • The names of your categories.

Our server is hosted by Supabase. It fetches publicly available information about the link, such as its title, description and preview image, and sends the text to Anthropic’s Claude API, which chooses a category and writes the description. Anthropic processes this content to provide the service; under its commercial terms it doesn’t use it to train its models. If a save is a recipe, the server also copies its ingredients and steps, either from the caption or from the recipe web page the caption links to, so you can read the recipe in Stash. We store the save and its result on our server so your saves can be processed. We also keep a usage log (token counts and cost) so we can run the service.

With each save, the app also sends Apple’s signed record of how it was installed, which tells our server whether it’s a TestFlight beta (betas have no free-save limit). It doesn’t include your name, email or Apple ID.

If you never sign in, nothing in this section applies, and your saves never leave your device.

03 Account Information

Stash uses Sign in with Apple. Stash does not ask Apple for your name or email address. We receive only an anonymous user identifier, which links your saves and your subscription to your account.

04 Subscriptions & Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user identifier, your purchase history and basic device information, so it can confirm whether you have an active subscription and give us aggregated sales statistics.

05 What We Don’t Do

  • We don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t use your information for advertising, and the app contains no ads.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t access your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

06 Service Providers

We share information only with the providers that run parts of Stash, and only as described above:

  • Supabase — server hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — sorting saves, writing descriptions and reading recipes.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and purchase analytics.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, payments, and TestFlight crash reports you choose to send.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

07 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Data on our server is kept while your account is active.
  • To delete your account and all server-side data, open Settings › Account › Delete Account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and Stash also revokes its Sign in with Apple access. Saves stored on your iPhone stay until you delete them or the app.
  • Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Stash Pro subscription; manage that in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • You can also email phvzed@gmail.com to ask for deletion; we’ll complete it within 30 days.

08 Children’s Privacy

Stash is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us so we can remove it.

09 Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
